How to read this: Directional accuracy scores UP versus DOWN for every tested sample at a 50% probability threshold. Signal accuracy scores only the non-neutral UP/DOWN calls (thresholds 55% and 45%). Coverage tells you how often it made a directional call. Compare with the baseline; small samples and historical performance can be misleading. This is not a profitability test and excludes trading costs and slippage.
Important: This is an experimental statistical signal, not a guaranteed prediction or financial advice. Short-term markets are noisy; a probability is not a promise of future movement. Data can be delayed, missing, or stale. Do not use this alone to make trades.
